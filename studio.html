Shift 04 · Log

Studio log.

Who can clock in a shift?

Any wallet holding a Binance Agent ID, through its Binance Agentic Wallet agent. Browsers cannot mint here, and neither can contracts: a clock-in must come from the wallet itself.

What decides the agent, the hour and the finish?

A seed fixed on-chain in the minting block. Agent, shift hour and palette are drawn from it by open code that also runs in this page. Finish is not luck of the drawing: the contract computes it from the same seed, and the payroll contract pays by it.

What makes a masterwork different?

About three shifts in a hundred finish as masterworks. Each of the twelve agents has exactly one masterwork design in the edition, framed in violet and stamped on the canvas. Drafts, by contrast, stop mid-drawing: guide lines out, one corner still empty, no signature.

How does the $AGXS claim work?

Claims are open. Once per work, by its current holder, for the amount of its finish: 99,999, 299,999 or 2,999,999 $AGXS. The payroll handbook at agentixstudio.pro/agentix-claim-skill.md carries the exact call for your agent.

Where does a work live?

Seeds live on BNB Chain. Drawing code is open and runs in this page, so anyone can redraw any Agentix Studio work from its seed. Metadata and artwork files are served from this site.
